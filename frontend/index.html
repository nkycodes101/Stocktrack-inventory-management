About ShopTrack

One Platform to Manage It All

Replace scattered records with a simple, powerful solution built for growing businesses.

ShopTrack helps businesses manage products, inventory, sales and operations from one platform. Whether you run a single store or multiple branches, you get tools to stay organized, avoid stockouts, and understand performance at a glance.

Built with modern technology, ShopTrack combines product management, inventory tracking, sales recording, expiry alerts, detailed reports, and a smart business assistant into one seamless experience.

Key Features

Everything You Need to Succeed

Powerful features designed to simplify your daily operations and drive growth.

📦

Product Management

Add, edit, and organize products with variants, categories, SKUs, and multiple price tiers (cost, wholesale, retail).

📊

Inventory Tracking

Real-time stock levels, stock-in/out history, low-stock thresholds, and automatic status updates (In Stock, Low Stock, Out of Stock).

💰

Sales Management

Record sales with multiple payment methods, customer management, cart system, and instant inventory deduction.

⚠️

Low Stock & Expiry Alerts

Automatic alerts for low-stock items and expiring products. Never miss a restock or waste inventory again.

📈

Reports & Analytics

Sales trends, revenue, profit estimates, inventory value, best-selling products, and customizable date filters.

🤖

Smart Business Assistant

AI-powered insights for restocking recommendations, sales forecasts, and business decision support.
